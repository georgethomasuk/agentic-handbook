# Slice · The safety team triages reports

## Goal

Every report lands in one inbox the safety team works from. Each member can see what is new, what a
colleague has picked up, and what is closed.

## Done-when

Demonstrated with two members of the safety team signed in at once: one picks a report up, and the
other sees it as taken without refreshing.

## Boundaries

- The inbox reads the canonical store. It holds no copy of a report.
- No export. What leaves the platform as a spreadsheet is the company's own handling (H5), and is
  not built here.
