# Feature · A named account for each analyst

## Goal

The insurer's analysts sign in as themselves. A shared login removes the one fact the company most
needs: which person saw a site's figures.

## Done-when

Each analyst has an account of their own, a shared credential cannot sign in, and the company can
remove one analyst without touching another.

## Status – no packet

Waits on the insurer naming who its analysts are.
