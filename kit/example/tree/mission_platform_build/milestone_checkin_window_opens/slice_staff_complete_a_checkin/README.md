# Slice · Staff complete a check-in on their own phone

## Goal

A member of staff opens the link they were sent, answers the agreed questions on their own phone,
and the answers reach the canonical store with nothing that says who gave them.

## Done-when

Demonstrated end to end on the narrowest supported phone: link, questions, submit, and the stored
check-in read back with no attributable field.

## Boundaries

- Nothing built for the check-in may make a check-in attributable to a person or a device (finding
  F-51, holding H3b).
- The wording of the questions is agreed outside the build. It is not changed here.
