# Feature · Send every member of staff an invitation

## Goal

Every member of staff gets one link, and the list of who was sent which link does not survive the
sending.

## Done-when

Every address on the staff list has been sent one invitation, and nothing stored afterwards pairs an
address with a token.

## Status – no packet

One question to settle when it is briefed: whether the staff list is read from the company's
directory or uploaded, since that decides where the list lives and for how long.
