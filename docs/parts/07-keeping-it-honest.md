# 7 · Keeping it honest

> **Draft order: 5th.** Retro rules, panel composition and the comment-budget evidence are documented.
> The framing around them is written fresh.

## The problem

Rules accumulate. An instructions file grows every time something goes wrong, and each addition makes
every other rule slightly less likely to be followed. Meanwhile a process register drifts in format
within days if nothing holds its shape.

And the central failure: **a rule the model is asked to check is not a check.**

## The mechanism

- **A retro register with two standing questions** — cheaper, and faster — a hard line ceiling, and a
  rule that you do not read the other entries before writing yours.
- **A three-occurrence bar** before anything is promoted into the standing constitution.
- **Enforcement over advice.** A rule that matters gets a hook or a CI check. A rule that does not
  is advice, and should be labelled as advice.
- **A review panel of named reviewer profiles**, seated per change against tiers of risk rather than
  run in full every time.
- **A defect-resolution fleet** where the loop is held by a shell script, not a model, and reviews are
  posted as comments rather than approvals.

## The rules that earned their place

- **Do not read the other entries.** Format drifted from three lines to a mean of forty-four in three
  days, because each writer matched the last.
- **Do not match the comment density of the file you are editing.** Imitation is the generator.
- **The model choosing to run a check is not the check running.** One rule sat in the instructions file
  unenforced, and the estate went from a low four-figure count of excess comment lines to a
  five-figure one. This is the single most persuasive number in the whole handbook. Re-read it.
- **Three occurrences is the bar.** One incident is not a rule.
- **Retire a register that is not paying for itself.** A gap register was closed after releasing none
  of its rows in twenty days.
- **A rewrite is not an improvement until it is measured.** The panel rewrite's cost effect is
  explicitly **not yet established** and is written down as not established.
- **Corrections are new commits, never amendments.**
- **The resolver never runs the validation.** The party doing the work does not grade the work.

## What it cost

Every figure here needs re-reading from source. The comment-budget before-and-after. The panel's
seats and refuters over the runs before the rewrite, and the cost-weighted share. The gap register's
row count and the twenty days.

Where a claim is not established, **say it is not established** — the source documents do, and it is
the most credible thing about them.

## The smallest version that works

A dated log with two standing questions, a rule that nothing joins the constitution until it has
happened three times, and one hook that enforces the rule you break most.
