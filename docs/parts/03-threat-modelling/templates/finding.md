# F-<n> · <one-line title, stated as the problem not the fix>

> **Finding** F-\<n\> · **Origin** \<taxonomy · category · element\> · **Raised** \<date\>

*What it is.* What the mechanism actually is, in plain language. A reader who knows the business but
not the architecture should follow this paragraph without help.

*Why it matters.* The consequence. Who is harmed, or what stops working. Size it honestly against
what happens today – if the current process is worse, say so.

*What was considered and set aside.* The controls that were proposed and not taken, each with its
reason. **This section is the point of the document.** A finding that shows only what was found reads
as a charge sheet; one that shows what was weighed invites correction.

*Treatment.* One word – mitigate · accept · share · investigate · refine spec. Anything needing
explanation goes in the next line, not here.

*Proposed response.* What would actually be done. Say plainly where a control is **not built**.

*Residual.* What is still true after the proposed response is done. A treatment with an empty residual
is rare and has to be earned.

*Owner.* Who holds it · *Status.* Raised / accepted / open
