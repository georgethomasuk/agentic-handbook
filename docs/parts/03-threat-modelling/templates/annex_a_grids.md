# Annex A · Coverage grids

> **Reference material, not reading material.** This exists so coverage can be *checked*. The
> narrative of what the pass found belongs in the workbook.

## How to read a cell

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `✅ F-n` | Produced a finding. The ID points to the entry in `annex_b/` |
| `➖` | Considered and closed. **The reason in the next column is the deliverable** – "n/a" is not a reason |
| `⚠️` | Cannot be closed here. Names what it waits on and who owns it |

**A `➖` is a result, not a blank.** A category considered and dismissed on stated grounds is a
decision. A category never considered is a gap. Distinguishing the two is the only thing this annex
is for.

**Every row carries a mark.** An unmarked row means the pass is incomplete.

## Finding-ID reservations

Each category claims a block **before its first cell**, so two sessions cannot both take the next free
number. Gaps are expected and cost nothing. Do not compact them.
